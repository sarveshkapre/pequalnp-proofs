# Proofs for small circuits, rectangle covers and sparse polynomials

These are the proof files for [Small Circuits](https://sarveshkapre.com/projects/p-vs-np/). Each package gives its input model, exact statement, prerequisites and replay instructions.

- [Boolean tables](table-circuits/README.md). Repeated rows and columns explain 104 exact circuit budgets. Two all-sizes families measure the cost of copying a wire or losing access to negation.
- [Five and six inputs](finite-certificates/mask-69/README.md). Lean checks lower bounds of seven and eight gates and verifies both known constructions.
- [Rectangle covers](finite-certificates/rectangle-cover/README.md). Two explicit fourteen-rectangle arrangements each miss two target cells. The restricted symmetry result explains which repairs can be ruled out.
- [Sparse polynomials](sparse-roots/README.md). Two trinomials can produce exactly eight distinct positive roots of one plus their product. The all-degree upper bound and a check of the earlier matching example are included.
