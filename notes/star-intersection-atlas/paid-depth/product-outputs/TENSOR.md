# Fixed-depth cost of independent copies

Let $R\subseteq X\times Y$ be a nonempty finite relation. Its $g$-fold tensor power accepts a pair of words exactly when every coordinate pair belongs to $R$. Let $\tau$ be the minimum weight of a fractional cover of the positive cells of $R$ by positive rectangles. Let $C_D$ and $F_D$ count paid intersections in a circuit and a formula at depth at most $D$. A formula charges every occurrence separately.

For fixed $D\ge1$, with $m=2^{D-1}$, both size measures have the same exponential rate:

$$
\lim_{g\to\infty} (1+C_D(R^{\otimes g}))^{1/g}
=\lim_{g\to\infty} (1+F_D(R^{\otimes g}))^{1/g}
=\tau^{1/m}.
$$

The fractional-cover characterization of amortized nondeterministic communication is classical. Here it determines the exponential rate in the binary-intersection model at each paid depth. The circuit lower bound permits reuse of all intermediate wires. The formula construction duplicates each intermediate result wherever necessary. [Karchmer, Kushilevitz and Nisan](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S0895480192238482) · [Cavalar and Oliveira](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2025/033/) · [Project depth conventions](../THEOREM.md)

**Fractional covers multiply.** The product of fractional covers is feasible. For the reverse inequality, use an optimal nonnegative dual weight on positive cells, of mass at most one on each positive rectangle. Product weights have total $\tau^g$. Any nonempty positive rectangle in the product projects at each coordinate to a positive rectangle of $R$, because every row in the rectangle crosses every column. The original rectangle is contained in the product of those projections, which has weight at most one.

**From depth to rectangles.** A circuit of $s$ gates has at most $(8s+1)^m$ bank members for its gate taps. A final raw row set and column set add at most two strips. Thus $\tau^g\le(8s+1)^m+2$ and $1+s\ge c_D\tau^{g/m}$. This is the lower bound for both star-circuit models. [Common bank](../../../../lean/PEqualNP/PaidDepth/CommonOutputBank.lean)

**From covers to a formula.** For a block of $h\ge1$ coordinates, normalize an optimal fractional cover of the product to a probability distribution. A given positive cell is covered with probability at least $\tau^{-h}$ on each independent draw. After $\lceil\tau^h(1+h\log |R|)\rceil$ draws the expected number of missed cells is less than one, so some choice covers them all. Purchase one depth-one gate per drawn rectangle and take their free union. Split $g$ into at most $m$ blocks with lengths differing by at most one, then take a balanced conjunction. The cost is at most $C_{D,R}(g+1)\tau^{g/m}$, without sharing. Taking $g$-th roots proves the assertion. If the integral and fractional minima coincide, a product of integral covers gives the same two-sided estimate up to constants without the factor $g+1$.

**A four-label example.** For ordinary disequality on four symbols, there are twelve positive ordered pairs. A positive rectangle has disjoint shores and at most four cells, so $\tau\ge3$. All six oriented balanced cuts of four symbols cover each positive cell twice. Weight one half on each gives $\tau=3$. The integral cover minimum is four. Three rectangles would all have size four and would partition the twelve cells. Each balanced rectangle contributes zero or two outgoing edges at a fixed label, so their disjoint union cannot supply its three outgoing edges. Four single-row rectangles suffice. The depth-two exponential rate for across-the-board disequality is therefore $\sqrt3$ per coordinate. Simply multiplying a smallest integral cover instead gives an upper rate of $2$ after balancing.

**Consequence for complementation.** If a fixed module of $k$ paid gates has one output, its rejected inputs have an integral cover by at most $2^k$ rectangles. To see this, choose one arm per gate and start by requiring every tapped gate and final raw signal to vanish. Whenever a required gate must vanish, forbid the raw signals on its chosen arm and recursively require every dependency on that arm to vanish. The surviving row and column choices form one rectangle. Every rejected input is included by choosing a vanishing arm whenever required. Conversely every resulting rectangle is rejected, by induction in the wiring order. The OR of $g$ disjoint copies has complement $R^{\otimes g}$ for this rejected relation. For fixed module and depth, a product of its integral covers on balanced blocks gives cost $O_{D,k}(2^{kg/m})$. A fixed repeated module cannot by itself raise the worst-case single-output exponent above the bitwise-inequality baseline.

For the universal raw-word model, the project's independent [negative rectangle-rank result](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/discussions/19729#discussioncomment-19919194) supplies an integral cover of size $J$ and a matching packing of $J$ witness cells. Whenever these two certificates are available, the rate above is exactly $J^{1/m}$ and the constant-factor estimate applies.
